Deriving the tokens

--acc-h and --acc-t are the accent darkened until text on #F4F7FB reaches 4.5:1. --acc-soft is the accent at about 8% on white; --acc-ring at 20% opacity. If white text on the accent is under 4.5:1, the set-up screen asks for a darker shade before it saves.
