01 — OUR STORY

Built for the systems nobody else wants to look after.

Most established businesses run on software someone built years ago. The developer moved on, the documentation never existed, and every small change feels risky. Meanwhile there's pressure to modernise and to use AI.

byteleaf exists to be the steady team behind those systems. We work directly with the business — no layers of account managers — fixing what's broken, keeping things updated, and building what's needed next, from the first screen to the last API call.
